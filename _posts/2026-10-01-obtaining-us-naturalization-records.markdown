
In my genealogy research I have once requested & received naturalization records
from the national archives, and now that I want to do it again for a few more
people -- I don't remember the first thing about how to do it!

So, for various families arriving at different times, I am going to document
the process here.  Most branches of my family's arrival predates the existence
of the U.S., so the ones I will start with are: the **Winters** family,
immigrated via a US-Canada crossing, and the **Dombrowski** and **Gerard**
families, both 19th century European ship crossings.

So the vague recollection I have is, find the crossing info, their residence
at time of naturalization, find the naturalization number somehow, and send
some money somewhere.  Let's embark!


## Winters, Jacob

My mother's father's family emigrated from Canada (Ontario to Upper Michigan)
in 1882ish, according to the 1900 Federal US Census.

Born 19 May 1874 @ Watt, Muskoka, Ontario, died 18 Dec 1902 @ Mathias, Alger,
Michigan.  He had a little lumber town up there in the UP, with a "Winters, MI"
post office and everything.

My mother's grandfather, my great-grandfather, this family was actually
relocated *to* Canada from New York State by the British after they fought as
Loyalists in the Revolutionary War.  Obtaining this documentation would be a
key element in requesting "Lost Canadian" proof of citizenship, which I
believe both my mother & I are eligible for since 2025 (but reading legal
things puts my brain in a kind of non-functional mode, so traversing that
process will probably be documented in another post)


## Gerard, Girard, Gérard, etc

My mother's mother's family.  I haven't done the research on this family for a
few years, I have a note attributed to "Biography of Charles L. Gerard" from
"Stark County Atlas, Ohio" that reads:

    Emigration
    1829 • USA

    (Jean) Georges Gérard (Charles L.' grandfather), with son Nicolas, his wife
    Frances and their children (and probably Nicolas' brothers) emigrated from
    France in 1829.

And on Ancestry I have a somewhat documented GGGGF called Nicolas Gérard born
14 Feb 1800 @ Lure, Haute-Saone, Franche-Comte, France who died 27 May 1879 @
Salt Creek Township, Wayne County Ohio.  Nicolas's wife was called Frances
Lingon (b. 30 Aug 1807 @ Nancy, Alsace Lorraine, France & d. 1888 @ Kansas
City), their son and my GGGF Frank was born 1837, maybe in New York.


## Dombrowski, John

According to 1910 U.S. Federal Census, arrived in 1893 from Poland, my
patrilineal great-grandfather (b. 10 May 1872 @ Warszawa, Mazowieckie, Poland
& d. 06 Jul 1914 @ Neenah, Winnebago, Wisconsin)
